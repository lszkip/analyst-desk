SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
- This resolver supports IBM's observed Exhibit 13 Items 7 and 8 layout.
  It is not a general external-reference crawler. Other references (including
  proxy statements) are not claimed complete. Market-risk material within the
  Item 7 pages is included under Item 7; it is not duplicated under Item 7A.
- Management/auditor pages between the two ranges, stockholder-information
  pages and performance graphs are outside these two Item references and are
  not added as Item 7/8 passages. All exhibit bytes remain available locally.
- The coverage warning remains a targeted short-Item check, not a complete
  audit of every referenced document. No warnings does not prove all possible
  financial research questions are answerable.
- Source HTML is never rewritten; scripts are not executed. This parser is
  not a browser/full XBRL engine and does not interpret external CSS.
- Whole tables are preserved, including presentation tables; no automatic
  financial-table classification or numeric-value conversion is claimed.
- Text chunks are at most 350 whitespace-delimited words by default. Tables
  remain whole; later retrieval needs to handle large table records.
- Keep one writer at a time. A failed batch preserves the preceding manifest
  and completed receipts for retry. Refresh is an explicit repair/rebuild,
  not a way to conceal unexplained verification failures.
- Supplement --refresh may re-download the selected exhibits. After changing
  those sources, rebuild the corresponding IBM passages with --refresh.
- Passage preparation and --verify-only always remain offline. Network access
  belongs only to the explicit supplement download command.
- untrusted_source is provenance metadata, not a complete prompt-injection
  defence. Model-facing tools must enforce their own trust boundaries.
