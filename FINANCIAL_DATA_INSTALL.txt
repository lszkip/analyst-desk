Prepared: 8 November 2026

WHAT WE FOUND

Your merged checkpoint is main, commit 022b714. The shared /ask lookup and
missing-year clarification are already installed. Your coverage report contains
135 requests: 15 companies x 3 fiscal years x 3 financial measures.

The report shows 128 ok, 4 not_found and 3 conflict. Salesforce's three conflicts
have the same cause: the SEC facts label two different annual periods FY2025.
The 2026 annual report's inline DocumentFiscalYearFocus also says 2025, but the
report explicitly defines fiscal 2026 as the year ending January 31, 2026.
Reading that inline tag again would not fix the conflict.

This update adds a shared, deliberately narrow rule that extracts an explicit
fiscal-year definition from an original filing. It validates company identity,
document type, annual focus, inline context and report date. It does not assume
the fiscal-year label equals the calendar year, and contains no company-specific
calendar or Salesforce exception. Unrecognized definitions remain unresolved.

The original document bytes, their SHA-256 fingerprints and extracted definition
are retained. Each lookup checks and reproduces the evidence from those bytes.
The underlying company-facts JSON is not rewritten by this repair.

LIMITS AND NEXT STEP

The extractor supports one explicit definition pattern, with minor wording and
HTML variations. It is not a general narrative/XBRL interpreter and cannot fix
every possible filing error. It never guesses a year from a month or date. If
it cannot establish a definition, the underlying conflict remains visible.

As-of filters use filing dates, not the date on which you downloaded the cache.
They exclude future filings and resolutions but do not reconstruct an older
version of SEC's online data from a newer snapshot.

Broadcom ProfitLoss and NetIncomeLoss differ in attribution. IBM's non-GAAP
operating earnings must not be silently substituted for GAAP operating income.
Those four missing cases need separate metric-definition work.

Coverage and byte fingerprints are not an independent accounting audit. These
checks do not replace the capstone golden evaluation or establish full support
for all questions about 15 companies.

A deliberate --refresh replaces snapshots. For Salesforce, include
--resolve-years 2025 with --refresh to rebuild the original-filing evidence too.
The collection of files is not one atomic transaction; a failed full refresh
can require a retry. Individual downloads are bounded to 25 MiB, use 30-second
timeouts and are spaced by at least 0.3 seconds in one process.
